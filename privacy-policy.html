VIDFLUENCER

Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal information when you visit our website and use our services.

We take your privacy seriously and are committed to being transparent about how we handle your data.

1. Who we are

Vidfluencer is a creator marketing and UGC agency operating as a partnership in the United Kingdom.

In this policy, “Vidfluencer”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the partnership operating under the Vidfluencer trading name.

2. What data we collect

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Account and contact information (name, email address, username).
  • Information about your interests and preferences.
  • Data about your use of our website (analytics and cookies).
  • Information you provide when you apply for campaigns or communicate with us.
  • Payment information (where applicable).
  • Information from third-party platforms (e.g. social media).

3. How we use your data

We use your information to:

  • Provide and manage your account and services.
  • Communicate with you and respond to your enquiries.
  • Match you with relevant campaigns.
  • Process payments (where applicable).
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Send relevant updates and marketing communications (where permitted).

4. Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to understand how our website is used, measure performance and improve your experience. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings or our cookie settings tool.

5. Sharing your information

We may share your information with:

  • Participating brands (where relevant to a campaign).
  • Service providers (e.g. website hosting, analytics, payment providers).
  • Legal and regulatory authorities (where required by law).

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Under 18s

We may accept creators under the age of 18. We take additional measures to protect the privacy and safety of younger users, including age verification, parental involvement where necessary and age-appropriate safeguards.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your data (where applicable).
  • Object to certain types of processing.
  • Withdraw consent (where applicable).

To exercise your rights, please contact us at Contact@vidfluencer.io.

8. Data retention

We keep your information only for as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this policy, or as required by law.

9. Security

We use appropriate security measures to protect your data from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. However, no system is completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on our website and will take effect when published.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at:

Contact@vidfluencer.io
