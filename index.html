Frequently asked

Good questions. Clear answers.

Still unsure about a campaign or the community? Get in touch and we’ll point you in the right direction.

How do creators join?

Join the Vidfluencer Discord and introduce yourself and the topics you create content about.

Is creator coaching free?

Yes. Creator coaching and community access are free. Campaign terms, including any payment, are set out before you take part.

Is there a follower minimum?

There is no general follower minimum to join. Individual campaign briefs may have their own requirements.

How do creators get paid?

Payment is made by PayPal, Wise or bank transfer, as agreed in the campaign terms.

What does it cost a brand?

Signing up and listing a campaign are free, with no subscription plan. Creator payments are agreed in the Brand Partnership Agreement. Vidfluencer adds no management charge on top of creator payments.

Who reviews campaign videos?

Vidfluencer reviews submitted videos against the agreed brief and manages creators on the brand’s behalf.
